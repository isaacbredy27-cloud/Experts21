# 06 — Permits, Licenses & Taxes

> Not legal advice. Fees and steps change, so confirm each one with the agency linked. The requirements also depend on which **municipality** the site is in: City of Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, or unincorporated Miami-Dade all differ.

## Who regulates what

| Level | Agency | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| State | **Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz)** | Form an LLC or corporation and register a fictitious name (DBA) if you use one. |
| Federal | **IRS** | EIN (employer ID). |
| State | **Florida Dept. of Revenue** | **Sales tax registration** (required before the DBPR license) and reemployment tax account (state unemployment tax, once you have employees). |
| State | **DBPR Division of Hotels & Restaurants** | **Public Food Service Establishment license.** Coffee shops fall here, not under the Florida Department of Agriculture (FDACS), which covers retail food stores. Includes plan review and an opening inspection. |
| State | **DBPR – Alcoholic Beverages & Tobacco** | Only if you serve beer or wine (2COP license) or more. Needs a zoning-approved Certificate of Use for alcohol. |
| County | **Miami-Dade Tax Collector** | **Local Business Tax Receipt (BTR)**, renewed every year Oct 1–Sept 30. If you're inside a city, you need **both** the city receipt **and** the county receipt. |
| County / City | **Certificate of Use (CU)** | Confirms the use is allowed by zoning at the address and the building is suitable. The City of Miami CU includes the **Fire Safety Permit** and inspections. Unincorporated and some municipal CUs go through Miami-Dade's EPS portal. |
| City / County | **Building permits** | For buildout (plumbing, electrical, mechanical, signage). Needs a licensed contractor and sealed drawings. Hurricane/wind-load and impact-glass code applies. |
| City | **Sidewalk café permit** (City of Miami Ch. 54, Art. VI) | For outdoor tables on the public sidewalk. Applied through iPW, with an inspection usually scheduled within 10 working days. |
| County | **DERM / Water & Sewer** | Grease interceptor and wastewater review if there is food prep. Environmental review may be needed for the site. |
| Federal / private | Music licensing (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC or a licensed background-music service) | Required for public background music. |

## DBPR food service license fees (from DBPR fee schedule)

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Application fee (new or change of ownership) | **$50** |
| Plan review | **$0** |
| Annual license, 1–49 seats | **$262** (half-year $136) |
| Annual license, 50–149 seats | $273 |

Florida also requires a **Certified Food Protection Manager** (the manager's certification) and **food handler training** for all employees within 60 days of hire.

## Taxes

| Tax | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Florida sales tax plus Miami-Dade surtax | **7%** (6% state + 1% county) | Applies to prepared food and beverages. |
| Miami-Dade 2% Food & Beverage Tax | 2% | Applies to establishments **licensed to sell alcohol for on-site consumption**, outside Miami Beach, Bal Harbour, and Surfside. A coffee-only café is likely exempt. **Confirm before adding beer or wine.** |
| Miami Beach resort taxes | Extra % on food and beverage | Miami Beach (and Bal Harbour and Surfside) levy their own resort taxes. Check if siting there. |
| Reemployment (unemployment) tax | Varies | Florida RT-6 filings each quarter. |
| Florida corporate income tax | 5.5% | C-corps only. LLCs taxed as partnerships pass through. |
| Tangible personal property tax | Varies | File DR-405 with the county property appraiser for equipment and furniture. |

## Suggested sequence and timeline (*est.*)

1. **Entity + EIN + bank account** (1–2 weeks).
2. **Zoning check** on candidate addresses *before signing* (City of Miami: e-start zoning lookup). Add a **permitting contingency** to the LOI/lease (rent starts only once permits are issued).
3. **Lease signed** and design/MEP drawings started.
4. **DBPR plan review** in parallel with **building permit** submittal (building permits often take 1–4+ months in Miami-Dade municipalities).
5. **Buildout** (2–4 months) with inspections.
6. **CU + Fire inspection**, **DBPR opening inspection**, **BTRs** (city + county), **sales tax** registration.
7. Optional: **sidewalk café permit**, **sign permit**, **alcohol license**.

**Typical total from signed lease to opening: 4–9 months.** Tie rent abatement to this.
