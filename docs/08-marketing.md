# 08 — Marketing

## Positioning hypotheses (to test)

1. **"Miami's specialty cafecito."** Specialty-grade Cuban-style drinks (colada, cortadito, café con leche) made with traceable Latin American single origins. Respects the local ritual and raises the quality. Priced between the ventanita and the third-wave café.
2. **"Fast specialty for the commute."** Specialty quality at chain speed. Mobile-order first, for Brickell and Downtown.
3. **"Latin American origins bar."** Rotating single origins from Colombia, Brazil, Honduras, Guatemala, Peru, and others, with producer stories. Connects to the city's diaspora and its Latin American tourists.

All three should be **bilingual**, built for iced drinks, and photogenic without being only "aesthetic." Miami coffee enthusiasts explicitly complain about cafés that are "just aesthetic with above average-mid" coffee.

## Pre-launch (8–12 weeks before opening)
- **Brand identity and Instagram/TikTok** live from lease signing. Document the buildout, coffee sourcing, and team. Short-form video showing brewing technique and origin stories works well with younger customers.
- **Pop-ups and coffee cart** at farmers markets, Wynwood and Little River events, coworking spaces, and run clubs to build a waitlist.
- **Founding-member program:** prepaid drink credits or memberships. Builds an audience and adds cash before opening.
- **Local press and food media:** Miami New Times, The Infatuation, Eater Miami, Time Out Miami, Miami Guide, plus local food creators.
- **Google Business Profile** created and verified before opening. Reviews drive "coffee near me" searches.

## Launch
- Soft opening (friends and family, neighbors, local businesses), then a grand opening with a free-colada hour, a latte-art throwdown, or a roaster collab.
- Timing: aim to **open in October–November**, ahead of peak season and Art Basel (early December). Avoid opening in July–September, the low tourist season and peak hurricane season.

## Ongoing channels

| Channel | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram / TikTok | Awareness, tourists, Gen Z | Bilingual captions. Reels of seasonal drinks. Collaborate with creators. |
| Google Maps / Yelp / TripAdvisor | Discovery, especially tourists | Ask for reviews, reply in English and Spanish. |
| Loyalty and app (Toast/Square loyalty, or a punch card) | Visit frequency | Reward the daily habit, e.g., "10th cafecito free." |
| Neighborhood partnerships | Locals | Gyms, coworking spaces, condo buildings (Brickell and Edgewater towers), offices, UM/FIU. |
| Corporate catering and wholesale | Off-peak revenue | Office coffee boxes, hotel lobby partnerships. |
| Events | Community | Cuppings, latte-art nights, Spanish/English coffee classes, Art Basel and Miami Music Week activations. |
| Retail beans and merch | Tourists, gifting | Packaging with a Miami identity. |

## Budget (*est.*)
- Pre-launch: $10k–$25k (brand, photography and video, website, events). See [04](04-startup-costs.md).
- Ongoing: **2–4% of sales**, higher in Year 1.

## KPIs
Transactions per day, average ticket, % mobile orders, loyalty members and repeat rate, Google rating (target 4.6+), review count, Instagram followers and engagement, catering/wholesale revenue share, and summer vs. peak revenue ratio.
