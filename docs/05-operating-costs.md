# 05 — Operating Costs & Unit Economics

## Rent by submarket (the biggest variable)

**Rule of thumb:** keep total occupancy cost (rent plus NNN) at or below **8–10% of sales**, and never above about 12%. Example: a 1,100 sf Brickell space at about $110/sf all-in costs about **$10k/month**, so it needs **about $100k/month in sales** to hit 10%. That is very ambitious for a single café.

## Monthly operating cost lines

| Line | Typical | Miami notes |
|---|---|---|
| COGS (coffee, milk, food, cups) | 28–32% of sales | Coffee drink gross margins run 65–85%. Food and alt-milks pull blended COGS up. Iced cups and lids cost more. |
| Labor including payroll taxes | 30–35% of sales | $15 Florida minimum wage as of 9/30/2026. See [07](07-staffing.md). |
| Occupancy (rent + NNN) | 8–12% target | See table above. |
| Card processing | 2.5–3.5% | About $750–$1,050/mo at $30k in sales. |
| Utilities | $1.5k–$3k/mo | HVAC load is high for most of the year. FPL electric. Water/sewer is Miami-Dade WASD. |
| Insurance (GL, property, workers' comp, liquor if any) | $600–$1,500/mo | Florida's BOP average is above the national average because of hurricane exposure. Wind/named-storm deductibles are often a percentage of insured value. |
| Marketing | 2–4% | Higher in the first year. |
| Repairs, maintenance, supplies, software | $1.5k–$3k/mo | Espresso machine service, water filters, POS and scheduling subscriptions, music licensing. |
| Accounting, legal, licenses | $300–$800/mo | Includes annual license renewals. |
| Debt service | varies | Example: $300k over 10 years at about 10% ≈ $4k/mo. |

Benchmarks: BizBuySell data shows average coffee shop revenue of about **$730k/yr** with about **9%** net margin. Healthy shops reach **10–20%** net margin. Most take **12–24 months** to become profitable.

## Break-even (*est.*)
- Fixed monthly costs (base labor floor, occupancy, utilities, insurance, admin, marketing, debt) are about **$36k** for a non-prime site and **$41k** for a prime site.
- Contribution margin after COGS and card fees is about **67%**.
- **Break-even revenue:** about **$54k/mo** (non-prime), or about 210 transactions/day at $8.50. About **$61k/mo** (prime), or about 240 transactions/day.

## Levers
- **Raise the average ticket:** food attach (pastry, toast, breakfast), size upsell, signature drinks, retail beans.
- **Wholesale and catering:** office catering in Brickell/Downtown, hotel and restaurant bean accounts. These offset summer softness.
- **Labor scheduling:** match staffing to the 7–10am peak and use a lean afternoon crew.
- **Menu engineering:** push high-margin espresso and cold brew. Watch alt-milk upcharges, which should cover their cost.
