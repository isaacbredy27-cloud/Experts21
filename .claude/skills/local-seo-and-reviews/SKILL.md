# Local SEO and reviews

## Steps
1. **Google Business Profile:** create and verify it before opening. Primary category "Coffee shop", secondary "Café" and "Espresso bar". Add hours (including holidays), attributes (Wi-Fi, outdoor seating, accessibility, Latino-owned if applicable), menu, 20+ photos, Q&A, and posts weekly.
2. **NAP consistency:** the same name, address, and phone everywhere (website, Yelp, TripAdvisor, Apple Maps, Instagram).
3. **Website:** a fast mobile page with the menu (as text, not only a PDF), hours, location, schema.org LocalBusiness markup, and bilingual content.
4. **Reviews:** ask happy customers (QR code at handoff and on receipts). Target 4.6+ stars. Reply to every review within 48h in the reviewer's language. Thank positive reviews specifically. For negative ones, acknowledge, take it offline, and fix the cause.
5. **Monitor:** rating, review count and velocity, search views, direction requests, calls. Track recurring complaint themes and feed them to operations.

## Output
A listing checklist, review reply drafts, and a monthly metrics summary.

## Guardrails
- Never buy, incentivize, or fake reviews (FTC rule on fake reviews, platform policies).
