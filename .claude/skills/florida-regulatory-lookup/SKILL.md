# Florida regulatory lookup

## Where things live
| Topic | Authority |
|---|---|
| Food service license, plan review, inspections | DBPR Division of Hotels & Restaurants (myfloridalicense.com) |
| Alcohol (2COP etc.) | DBPR Alcoholic Beverages & Tobacco |
| Sales tax, reemployment tax | Florida Dept. of Revenue (floridarevenue.com) |
| Entity, fictitious name | Sunbiz (Division of Corporations) |
| Minimum wage | Fla. Const. Art. X §24; Fla. Stat. 448.110; FloridaCommerce |
| Local Business Tax Receipt | Miami-Dade Tax Collector and the city |
| Certificate of Use, zoning | City (e.g., miami.gov, Miami 21 code) or Miami-Dade RER/EPS for unincorporated areas |
| Building permits | City or county building department |
| Environmental (grease, wastewater) | Miami-Dade DERM / WASD |
| County ordinances | Miami-Dade Code (Municode) |

## Steps
1. **Identify the jurisdiction first.** Which municipality is the address in? City of Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, and unincorporated county all differ.
2. Go to the official page or statute. Quote the exact rule, fee, and effective date.
3. Cross-check against `docs/06-permits-licenses.md` and note any changes.
4. Cite with the URL and access date.

## Output
Rule, citation, fee or timeline, what it means for us, and any open question for an agency call.

## Guardrails
- Not legal advice. Flag when a licensed attorney or CPA should confirm.
- If official sources conflict or are unclear, say so and recommend calling the agency.
