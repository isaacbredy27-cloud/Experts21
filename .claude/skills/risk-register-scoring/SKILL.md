# Risk register scoring

## Baseline register
See `docs/09-risks.md`. The top risks are occupancy cost, permitting delays, undercapitalization, hurricanes and insurance, labor, green coffee prices, seasonality, saturation, the cafecito price anchor, parking, regulatory change, and climate/utilities.

## Steps
1. List the risks specific to the decision at hand. Start from the baseline and add new ones.
2. Score **likelihood** (1–5) and **impact** (1–5). Score = L × I. 15 or more is critical, 8–14 is high, under 8 is monitor.
3. For each risk above 8: mitigation, owner agent, trigger or early warning signal, and residual score.
4. Name the **top 3** that would change the decision and what evidence would retire each one.

## Output
| Risk | L | I | Score | Mitigation | Owner | Trigger | Residual |

## Guardrails
- Be specific ("rent above 12% of sales at Site B"), not generic ("rent risk").
