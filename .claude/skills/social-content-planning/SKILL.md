# Social content planning

## Content pillars
1. **Craft:** brewing technique, latte art, dial-in, origin stories (short-form video works best).
2. **Miami culture:** cafecito rituals, neighborhood life, bilingual humor.
3. **People:** baristas, producers, regulars.
4. **Product and offers:** seasonal and iced drinks, new pastries, events.
5. **Behind the build** (pre-launch): lease, buildout, equipment arrival, tastings.

## Steps
1. Set goals and KPIs: followers, reach, saves and shares, waitlist sign-ups, foot traffic from promos.
2. Build a 4-week calendar: 3–5 Reels/TikToks and 2–3 posts per week, daily stories. Bilingual captions.
3. Plan around key dates: Art Basel (early Dec), Miami Music Week (March), Calle Ocho (March), National Coffee Day (Sept 29), Hispanic Heritage Month (Sept 15–Oct 15), and hurricane season messaging.
4. For each item: hook (first 2 seconds), shot list, caption EN/ES, hashtags, CTA.
5. Review weekly: top performers, then do more of that.

## Output
A content calendar table (date, pillar, format, hook, caption EN/ES, CTA, owner) and the KPI targets.
