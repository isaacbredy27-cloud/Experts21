# Customer research

## Steps
1. **State the hypothesis to test**, for example "Brickell commuters will pay $5 for a specialty cortadito if served in under 3 minutes."
2. **Pick a method:**
   - Intercept interviews (10–20, at target corridors, 7–10am)
   - Short online survey (8–12 questions, bilingual EN/ES)
   - Social listening (Reddit r/Miami and r/SpecialtyCoffee, Google and Yelp reviews of competitors, Instagram comments)
3. **Write the questions** neutrally and bilingually. Include current behavior (where, how often, spend), unmet needs, willingness to pay (Van Westendorp or simple price ladders), and concept reaction.
4. **Analyze:** segment results by neighborhood, age, and language. Code open-ended answers into themes and count them.
5. **Conclude:** is the hypothesis supported, rejected, or unclear? What would change the decision?

## Output
Method, sample (n, where, when), key findings with counts or percentages, quotes (translated if needed), and the decision implication.

## Guardrails
- Small samples point a direction but don't prove it. Say so.
- Don't collect personal data you don't need.
