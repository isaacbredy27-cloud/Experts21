# Tax compliance

## Key rates and obligations (docs/06; verify)
- **Sales tax 7%** in Miami-Dade (6% state + 1% county surtax) on prepared food and drinks.
- **Miami-Dade 2% Food & Beverage tax** applies to establishments licensed for on-premises alcohol consumption (outside Miami Beach, Bal Harbour, Surfside). It likely doesn't apply to a coffee-only café. Re-check if adding beer or wine.
- **Miami Beach, Bal Harbour, and Surfside** levy their own resort taxes on food and beverage.
- **Payroll:** federal withholding, FICA, FUTA, and Florida reemployment tax (RT-6, quarterly).
- **Tangible personal property:** DR-405 return to the Property Appraiser by April 1.
- **Local Business Tax Receipts:** city and county, renewed each year by Sept 30 (penalties start in October).
- **Corporate income tax:** 5.5% for C-corps. LLCs are pass-through by default.

## Steps
1. Confirm the jurisdiction and the entity type.
2. List every tax with rate, base, filing frequency, due date, and agency.
3. Build a compliance calendar for months 1–12.
4. Estimate cash impact (for example, sales tax collected is not revenue; hold it separately).

## Output
A tax table and a compliance calendar.
