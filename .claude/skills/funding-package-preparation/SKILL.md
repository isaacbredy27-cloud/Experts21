# Funding package preparation

## Capital options (docs/04)
SBA 7(a) (most common, 10–30% equity injection), SBA microloan (up to $50k), equipment financing or leasing, landlord TI, friends and family or angel equity, convertible notes, presales and memberships, local CDFIs (community lenders).

## Package contents
1. Executive summary (concept, site, team, ask, use of funds).
2. Market and customer analysis (docs/01–03).
3. Concept, menu, and positioning.
4. Operations and staffing plan.
5. Marketing plan.
6. Management bios and resumes (industry experience matters a lot to lenders).
7. **Financials:** sources and uses, a 3-year monthly projection for year 1 and annual for years 2–3, break-even, DSCR (lenders want at least 1.25×), sensitivity.
8. Personal financial statement, credit, tax returns (owner), collateral, signed LOI or lease.
9. SBA forms (Form 1919, 413) as applicable.

## Steps
1. Build sources and uses from `capex-budgeting`.
2. Pull projections from `financial-modeling`. Compute DSCR in the base and conservative cases.
3. Pick the funding mix and compare cost of capital, dilution, and personal guarantee exposure.
4. Draft the plan and a checklist of missing documents.

## Output
A sources and uses table, the funding mix recommendation, the plan draft, and the document checklist.
