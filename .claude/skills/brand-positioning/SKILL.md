# Brand positioning

## Candidate positions (docs/08)
1. "Miami's specialty cafecito": specialty Cuban-style drinks, priced between the ventanita and third-wave cafés.
2. "Fast specialty for the commute": specialty quality at chain speed, mobile-first.
3. "Latin American origins bar": rotating Latin American single origins with producer stories.

## Steps
1. **Target:** the primary segment and secondary segment (docs/02).
2. **Competitive frame:** who the customer would otherwise choose (docs/03).
3. **Positioning statement:** "For [target] who [need], [brand] is the [frame] that [benefit] because [reason to believe]."
4. **Proof points:** 3 concrete, operational proofs (for example, "under 3-minute ticket times", "a single-origin colada", "producer named on every bag").
5. **Voice:** 3 adjectives, with do and don't examples in English and Spanish.
6. **Test:** does it work for a commuter, a local abuela, and a tourist? Does it fit the chosen site?

## Output
A positioning statement, proof points, a voice guide, and the risks of this position.

## Guardrails
- Avoid "just aesthetic". Miami enthusiasts call that out. Back every claim with operations.
