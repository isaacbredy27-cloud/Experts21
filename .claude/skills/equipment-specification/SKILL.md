# Equipment specification

## Steps
1. **Volume targets:** peak drinks per hour (for example 80–120 at the 7–10am peak), the menu mix (iced share is often over 50% in Miami *est.*), and food program needs.
2. **Espresso:** group count (2 groups for up to about 250 drinks/day, 3 for higher peaks), multi-boiler/PID, volumetric controls. Brands: La Marzocco, Slayer, Synesso, Victoria Arduino. Budget $15–30k.
3. **Grinders:** 2 espresso grinders (house plus decaf or single origin) plus 1 bulk grinder. $5–12k.
4. **Brew:** batch brewer, cold brew system (sized for daily volume), pour-over station if on the menu.
5. **Ice:** size for iced-heavy volume (about 4–8oz of ice per iced drink). Typically 300–500 lb/day production plus storage bin. Air-cooled vs. remote condenser given the heat.
6. **Refrigeration:** under-counter at the bar (milk), reach-in, pastry display case.
7. **Water:** filtration and softening for Miami's hard water to protect equipment (scale is a top failure cause).
8. **Utilities:** power (208/240V, amps per item), dedicated circuits, water and drain lines, floor drains, venting. Feed this to the architect.
9. **Service:** a local technician with response time, and a preventive maintenance schedule.

## Output
An equipment list (item, spec, qty, power, water/drain, footprint, budget, lead time) and the utility requirements summary.
