# Insurance and flood analysis

## Steps
1. **Flood zone:** look up the address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center (msc.fema.gov) or the Miami-Dade flood zone viewer. Zones AE/VE are high risk, X is moderate to low. Note the base flood elevation and the storefront elevation if known.
2. **Coverage stack for a café:**
   - General liability (Florida small business about $700–2,500/yr)
   - Property / BOP (about $900–3,000/yr; wind coverage is often subject to a named-storm deductible of 2–5% of insured value)
   - Flood (NFIP or private; separate policy)
   - Business interruption / income, including civil authority and utility service interruption
   - Workers' comp (required at 4+ employees)
   - Equipment breakdown, spoilage, cyber, EPLI, liquor liability if serving alcohol
   - Landlord-required limits from the lease
3. **Quotes:** get them from 2–3 brokers experienced with Florida hospitality. Compare premium, deductibles (especially named-storm), exclusions, and sublimits.
4. **Scenario:** estimate the loss from a 2-week hurricane closure (lost gross profit + spoilage + fixed costs) vs. what coverage pays after deductibles.

## Output
Flood verdict, a coverage table (coverage, limit, deductible, premium, insurer), the hurricane scenario, and gaps.

## Guardrails
- Premium figures are ballpark until quoted.
