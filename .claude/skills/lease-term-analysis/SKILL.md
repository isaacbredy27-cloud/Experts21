# Lease term analysis

## Checklist (café tenant in Miami)
- **Term and options:** 5–10 years plus renewal options. Align with how long the buildout takes to pay back.
- **Rent start:** tie it to permit issuance or opening, not to signing. Ask for 3–6 months of free rent after delivery.
- **Contingencies:** zoning and use approval, building permit, DBPR plan approval, financing. Make the lease voidable if these aren't obtained in about 120–180 days.
- **TI allowance:** $/sf, when it's paid out (at opening, not after 12 months), and the landlord's work letter (HVAC, electrical, plumbing stub-outs, grease interceptor).
- **Escalations:** fixed % (about 3%) vs. CPI. Cap NNN/CAM increases (for example 5%/yr, with controllable expenses excluded).
- **Use clause:** broad enough for coffee, food, retail beans, and optional beer/wine. **Exclusive** on specialty coffee in the center.
- **Personal guarantee:** limit it (burn-off after 24–36 months, or capped at months of rent).
- **Co-tenancy, relocation, and radius restrictions:** strike or limit them.
- **Signage, outdoor seating, hours, and venting rights.**
- **Hurricane and casualty:** rent abatement during closure, restoration timelines, and the insurance requirements on the tenant.
- **Assignment/sublet** to allow a future sale of the business.

## Steps
1. Extract every term into a table (term, rent, escalations, NNN, TI, free rent, contingencies, guarantee, exclusives, casualty).
2. Rate each term: favorable, market, or unfavorable, with a reason.
3. Propose redlines in priority order: must-have, nice-to-have, tradeable.

## Output
A term table, a redline list, and the effective rent over the term (with `rent-and-occupancy-analysis`).

## Guardrails
- This is analysis, not legal advice. A licensed Florida attorney approves the final documents.
