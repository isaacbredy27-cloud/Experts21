# B2B proposal writing

## Offer types
- **Office catering:** coffee boxes (96oz, about 10–12 cups), pastry platters, coffee bar pop-ups for events.
- **Recurring office coffee:** weekly beans plus equipment guidance.
- **Hotels and restaurants:** wholesale beans (private label if roasting partner allows), barista training.
- **Events:** mobile espresso cart (needs the appropriate DBPR license and event permits).

## Steps
1. Qualify the prospect: size, current supplier, pain points, decision maker, budget.
2. Price: cost-plus with a target margin (catering 60–70% gross margin, wholesale beans about 30–40% *est.*), delivery fees, minimums, and volume tiers.
3. Proposal: problem, offer, menu or options, pricing table, service levels (lead time, delivery window), terms (payment net 15/30, cancellation), and references.
4. Follow up and track pipeline (stage, value, next step, date).

## Output
A proposal document, a pricing table, and a pipeline tracker entry.

## Guardrails
- Check capacity first, so peak café service isn't compromised.
