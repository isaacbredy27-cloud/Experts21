# Menu engineering and recipe costing

## Steps
1. **Recipe card per item:** ingredients with quantity × unit cost (coffee at about 18–20g per double shot; milk oz; syrup; cup, lid, sleeve, straw; garnish). Include waste (3–5%).
2. **Cost % = item cost ÷ net price** (price before 7% sales tax). Targets: espresso drinks 15–25%, food 28–35%, blended café 28–32%.
3. **Price** using cost targets, benchmarks (`price-benchmarking`), and positioning. Use charm pricing carefully. Set alt-milk and extra-shot upcharges to cover cost.
4. **Menu matrix:** classify items by popularity × contribution margin into Stars, Plowhorses, Puzzles, and Dogs. Promote Stars, reprice Plowhorses, reposition Puzzles, and cut Dogs.
5. **Miami-specific:** iced versions of every core drink (iced cups and lids cost more), a value tier (specialty cafecito or colada), and Cuban-style signatures (cortadito, café con leche) made with specialty beans.
6. **Sensitivity:** show the effect of ±$1/lb green coffee and ±10% dairy on cost %.

## Output
A recipe cost table (item, cost, price, cost %, margin $), the menu matrix, and recommended changes.
