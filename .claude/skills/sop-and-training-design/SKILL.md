# SOP and training design

## SOP format
```
# SOP: <task>
Purpose · Owner · Frequency · Tools/materials
Steps (numbered, one action each, with standard: time/temp/weight)
Quality check · Common mistakes · Photo/visual reference
```

## Core SOP set for the café
Opening, closing, espresso dial-in and calibration, milk steaming, drink build specs (each menu item), iced drink builds, cold brew batch, batch brew, mobile order handoff, cash handling and POS, cleaning (machine backflush, grinder, ice machine), food safety logs, receiving deliveries, hurricane preparation and closure.

## Training curriculum
1. Week 1: culture, safety, food handler certificate, POS, register, and handoff.
2. Week 2: espresso fundamentals, milk, and the core menu with a practical sign-off.
3. Week 3: speed drills, rush simulation, iced and signature drinks, customer service in English and Spanish.
4. Ongoing: weekly cupping and calibration. Optional SCA modules for leads.

## Output
SOP documents (bilingual where staff need it), checklists, and a training plan with sign-off criteria.
