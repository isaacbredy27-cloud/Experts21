# Zoning and permit planning

## Steps
1. **Jurisdiction and zoning:** find the municipality and zoning district for the address (City of Miami: Miami 21 transect, checked via the city's zoning lookup). Is food service or a café allowed **by right**, conditionally, or not at all?
2. **Prior use:** was it a restaurant or café before (2nd-gen)? Does the existing CU cover food service? A change of use can trigger impact fees, parking requirements, and a full building review.
3. **Permit map:** list each permit with agency, prerequisites, typical duration, and fee:
   - Entity, EIN, sales tax registration → DBPR plan review → building permits (MEP, signage) → buildout inspections → Certificate of Use + fire inspection → DBPR opening inspection and license → city and county BTR → optional sidewalk café, sign, and alcohol permits.
4. **Red flags:** no grease interceptor where food prep is planned, an inadequate ADA restroom, a historic district, flood zone elevation rules, or parking requirements.
5. Feed durations to `project-scheduling`.

## Output
A zoning verdict, a permit table (permit, agency, prerequisite, duration, fee, status), red flags, and questions for a pre-application meeting.

## Guardrails
- Verify on official sources. Recommend a pre-application meeting with the city for anything unclear.
