# Space planning and workflow

## Principles
- **Linear flow:** door → menu view → order → pay → bar → handoff → condiments → seating or exit. Queues mustn't block the entrance or seating.
- **Bar zones:** espresso at the center, grinders on the barista's dominant side, milk fridge under the steam wand, cups within reach, ice and cold brew on the iced station, and handoff at the end of the bar facing customers.
- **Mobile orders:** a separate, visible pickup shelf near the door so app customers bypass the queue.
- **Seating mix:** for a commuter site, mostly grab-and-go plus counter seats. For a neighborhood or remote-work site, more 2-tops, outlets in specific zones, and some communal tables. Outdoor seating needs shade.
- **Back of house:** dry storage, dish, mop sink, a handwash sink at each station (food code), and an ADA restroom.
- **Climate:** an air curtain or vestibule at the entrance, and keep heat-producing equipment away from seating.

## Steps
1. Get the space dimensions, utilities locations, and storefront.
2. Allocate area (typical café: about 35–45% back of house and bar, 55–65% front of house).
3. Sketch 2 layout options (describe them or draw ASCII) with flow arrows. Count seats.
4. Check ADA clearances (36" paths, 60" turning circle), egress, and handwash sink placement.
5. Estimate peak throughput and identify bottlenecks.

## Output
Layout options with seat counts, flow notes, and the pros and cons of each.
