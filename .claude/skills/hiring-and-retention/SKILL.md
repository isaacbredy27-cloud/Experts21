# Hiring and retention

## Market (docs/07)
Barista $15–23/hr plus tips (about $2–6/hr *est.*). Shift lead $18–24/hr. GM $55–75k + bonus. Florida minimum wage is $15. Bilingual EN/ES is essential, and PT is a plus near tourist areas.

## Steps
1. **Role cards:** for each role, the mission, responsibilities, must-haves (bilingual, availability for 6–7am opens), nice-to-haves, and pay band.
2. **Sourcing:** Indeed, Culinary Agents, Instagram, specialty coffee community, culinary schools (Miami Dade College), referral bonus.
3. **Interview guide:** structured questions, a practical bar test for experienced hires, and a scoring rubric.
4. **Onboarding:** paperwork (I-9, E-Verify if 25+ employees, W-4), food handler training, the training curriculum, and a 30/60/90-day check-in.
5. **Retention:** pay above the minimum, a predictable schedule, a tip program, a career ladder (barista → lead → trainer → manager), free drinks and shift meals, a transit or parking stipend, and an education budget.
6. Track turnover, time-to-hire, and the reasons people leave.

## Output
Job descriptions, an interview kit, an onboarding checklist, and a retention plan with costs.
