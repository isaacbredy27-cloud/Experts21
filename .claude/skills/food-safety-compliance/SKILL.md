# Food safety compliance

## Requirements (DBPR Division of Hotels & Restaurants)
- At least one **Certified Food Protection Manager** (ServSafe or another approved program). All employees get **food handler training** within 60 days of hire (DBPR-approved provider).
- **Plan review** before opening or remodeling. **Opening inspection** before licensing. Routine unannounced inspections after that.
- Florida uses the FDA Food Code with Florida amendments (Ch. 61C-4, F.A.C., and 509 F.S.).

## Key controls for a café
- Cold holding at 41°F or below (milk, alt-milks, dairy pastries). Log fridge temps twice a day.
- Hot holding at 135°F or above if any hot food is held.
- Date-marking for ready-to-eat items (7-day rule). Cold brew handling and shelf life.
- A handwash sink at each station with soap, towels, and signage. No bare-hand contact with ready-to-eat food.
- Allergen labeling and controls (milk, nuts in syrups or pastries, soy, oat/gluten).
- Ice machine cleaning and sanitizing (a common violation). Sanitizer concentration test strips.
- Pest control contract and logs. Hurricane season: a power-loss food discard policy (over 4 hours above 41°F).

## Steps
1. Build checklists for opening, closing, and weekly tasks.
2. Map menu items to hazards and controls (HACCP-lite).
3. Prepare for inspection with a self-audit against the DBPR inspection form.

## Output
Checklists, a hazard/control table, and the training plan.
