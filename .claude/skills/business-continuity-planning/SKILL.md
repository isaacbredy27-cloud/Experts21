# Business continuity planning (hurricane-first)

## Phases
1. **Season prep (by June 1):** confirm insurance, photograph and inventory equipment, test shutters and impact glass, keep a generator or transfer switch plan, stock sandbags, back up the POS and records to the cloud, update the staff contact tree, and agree a landlord protocol.
2. **Watch (48–72h):** reduce perishable orders, top up cash reserve access, brief staff, secure outdoor furniture and signage.
3. **Warning (24–36h):** close per the evacuation order, raise equipment off the floor, unplug, empty and prop open fridges if a long outage is expected, shut water to the espresso machine, install shutters, take photos.
4. **Recovery:** safety check (power, water, mold), document damage, file claims within policy deadlines, discard food per the food safety rules (over 4 hours above 41°F), re-sanitize the ice machine and water lines, flush filters, and reopen in stages. Tell customers on social media.
5. **Staff care:** pay policy during closures and check-ins on wellbeing.

## Output
A checklist per phase with owners, the cost of preparation items, and a closure cash plan.
