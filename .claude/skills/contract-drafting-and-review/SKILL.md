# Contract drafting and review

## Review checklist
Parties and entity names (match Sunbiz) · scope and deliverables · price and payment terms · term, renewal, termination (for convenience and for cause) · warranties · limitation of liability and indemnity · insurance requirements · IP ownership (brand, recipes, photos) · confidentiality · non-solicit or non-compete (Florida enforceability, Fla. Stat. 542.335) · force majeure (hurricanes) · dispute resolution and venue (Miami-Dade) · governing law (Florida) · auto-renewal traps · personal guarantees.

## Common café contracts
Equipment lease or finance, roaster/supply agreement (pricing, minimums, exclusivity), POS/processing (term, early termination fee), contractor/GC (schedule, change orders, retainage, lien waivers under Florida construction lien law), employment offer letters and policies, partnership/operating agreement, catering and wholesale terms, creator or influencer agreements (FTC disclosure).

## Steps
1. Summarize the contract in plain English (one paragraph).
2. Table: clause, what it says, risk (H/M/L), recommended change.
3. Draft redlines or a template as requested.

## Output
A summary, the risk table, and redlines or a draft.

## Guardrails
- Not legal advice. A licensed Florida attorney reviews before signing.
