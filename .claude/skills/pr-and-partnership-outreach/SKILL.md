# PR and partnership outreach

## Targets
- **Media:** Miami New Times, Eater Miami, The Infatuation Miami, Time Out Miami, Miami Herald food, Miami Guide, neighborhood newsletters, Spanish-language media (el Nuevo Herald, Univision and Telemundo local).
- **Creators:** Miami food and coffee creators (micro, 5–50k followers, often more effective), bilingual creators.
- **Partners:** coworking spaces, gyms and run clubs, condo towers (Brickell, Edgewater), offices, hotels, UM and FIU, farmers markets, local bakeries and roasters, art galleries (Wynwood), events (Art Basel satellites).

## Steps
1. Build a target list: name, outlet or organization, contact route, angle, last relevant coverage, priority.
2. Find the **story angle**: what's new and local (a specialty cafecito, the founder's story, Latin American producers, a community mission).
3. Draft pitches (under 150 words, personalized, bilingual where relevant) and a press kit (fact sheet, photos, founder bio, menu).
4. Partnership proposals: mutual value, what each side gives, how it's measured, how long it runs.
5. Sequence: pre-launch exclusives, then the soft opening invite list, then the grand opening, then ongoing events.

## Output
A target list table, pitch drafts, a press kit outline, and partnership one-pagers.
