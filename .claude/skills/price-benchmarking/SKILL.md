# Price benchmarking

## Known 2026 Miami ladder (docs/03)
| Item | Ventanita | Specialty |
|---|---|---|
| Cafecito/espresso | $1–2 | $3.25–4 |
| Colada | ~$1.50–3.30 | (gap) |
| Cortado/cortadito | ~$2.50–4.40 | $4.50–5.50 |
| Pour-over | — | $5–7 |
| Latte | — | $5.50–8 |

## Steps
1. Pick 6–10 comparable competitors (same neighborhood and format).
2. Capture prices for a standard basket: espresso, cortado, 12oz latte, iced latte, cold brew, pour-over, a croissant, an alt-milk upcharge, and a 12oz bag of beans. Use online menus or delivery apps (delivery prices are often 10–20% higher, so note it).
3. Build a table and compute the median and range per item.
4. Place the proposed price for each item. State whether it sits below, at, or above the median, and why.

## Output
A benchmark table, then the proposed price position and the margin impact (hand off to `menu-engineering-and-recipe-costing`).

## Guardrails
- Record the date and source (in-store, website, or delivery app) for each price.
