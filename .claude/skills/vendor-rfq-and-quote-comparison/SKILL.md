# Vendor RFQ and quote comparison

## Steps
1. **Scope:** write a clear spec (quantities, models or performance specs, service level, delivery date, site address). Use `equipment-specification` for equipment.
2. **Vendor list:** 3 or more qualified vendors, preferably local to Miami-Dade for service response. Check licenses (DBPR for contractors), reviews, and references.
3. **RFQ document:** scope, required line-item pricing, payment terms, lead time, warranty, service/SLA, insurance certificate, and the response deadline.
4. **Normalize bids** into one table with the same units. Note exclusions and allowances.
5. **Score:** price (total cost of ownership, not just the sticker), lead time, service and warranty, references, and risk. Weight according to the decision.
6. **Negotiate:** use the competing bids. Ask for bundle discounts, extended warranty, and installation or training included.

## Output
A comparison table, scoring, a recommended vendor with the reason, and negotiation points.

## Guardrails
- Don't share one vendor's pricing with another verbatim. Use ranges.
- Verify contractor licenses at myfloridalicense.com.
