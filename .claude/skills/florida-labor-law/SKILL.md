# Florida labor law

## Current rules (verify each year)
- **Minimum wage $15.00/hr from 9/30/2026.** CPI-indexed each Sept 30 from 2027.
- **Tipped employees:** cash wage at least $11.98 (fixed $3.02 tip credit). Tips must bring the total to at least $15. The employer must make up any shortfall.
- **Overtime:** 1.5× over 40 hours/week (FLSA). Florida has no daily overtime.
- **Tip pools:** managers and supervisors (and owners) can't keep tips (FLSA 2018 amendments, DOL rules). Back-of-house can join if no tip credit is taken.
- **Workers' comp:** required at 4+ employees (non-construction).
- **E-Verify:** required for private employers with 25+ employees (Fla. Stat. 448.095). I-9 for everyone.
- **Minors:** Florida child labor hour limits for 16–17-year-olds and under-16s.
- **Postings:** Florida minimum wage notice and federal posters.
- **Food handler training** within 60 days of hire. At least one Certified Food Protection Manager.
